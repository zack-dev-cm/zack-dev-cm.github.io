Why the change matters

NaN defeats a distance-only check.

The original check asks whether a returned coordinate moved by more than one pixel. For the undefined neighbor, abs(NaN − 1) > 1 is false, so the invalid mapping survives.

The proposed change also rejects a nonfinite inverse coordinate. Pixel 1 then becomes NaN with a zero footprint, while pixel 0 keeps value 1. The two modes here replay actual Python outputs from the same two-pixel fixture.
