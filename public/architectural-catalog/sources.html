Source notes

Catalog facts, visible in 3D.

The study reuses the original documents recovered with the architectural matching project. The Case Systems product catalog has 306 pages; B3000 and B3100 appear on pages 27 and 28. Their diagrams establish one full-width upper drawer versus two side-by-side upper drawers. Both entries list two hinged doors and one adjustable shelf.

B3000 is a visible callout on page 1 of the original elevation template; B3100 appears on page 3. Minimal original callout and catalog crops are reused from the published functional demo. The complete sheets stay in the project workspace. Procedural cabinet models explain the family geometry.

Dimensions and geometry

The initial 36 W × 34 H × 24 D inch size is a catalog-allowed nominal configuration. The width control uses a reviewed subset of options available for both families. These are selected catalog sizes. Installed dimensions, physical quantities and prices remain unknown.

Finish, hardware shape, panel construction, clearances and door/drawer motion are authored to make inspection readable. The downloadable GLB uses metres, with the nominal inch configuration in its metadata. It is a visualization, not a fabrication model.

Source and verification

The reviewed fact record identifies the recovered catalog by SHA-256. The browser verifies that record's checksum before showing the scene. The manufacturer's catalog resources provide the current edition, which may have different pagination and specifications.

The scene uses locally hosted Three.js r180 and OrbitControls. The 64-second film is captured from the same scene at fixed times. Eight stages explain the original drawing, recorded native-text extraction, exact catalog match, inspection, agent review, actual recorded ImageGen concept, illustrative room fit and evidence handoff. This film explains recorded examples; the working full-flow demo runs the agent jobs.

Recorded evidence and room fit

The workflow evidence record preserves original image hashes, PDF page/coordinate extraction, reviewed match and recorded Codex producer information for both examples. The browser verifies the record and all six images before displaying the workflow. ImageGen images are the recorded nominal 36-inch oak concepts; changing the procedural model does not change those recorded images.

The workroom includes two adjoining 24-inch cabinet variants, a continuous counter, upper storage, a worktable and two chairs, with a courtyard beyond the window. These surroundings are authored illustrations. Match bay to cabinet sets the opening equal to the selected nominal width using 25.4 mm per inch. Wider openings close their side gaps with illustrative infill; an oversized cabinet stops in front of the run. This 3D control checks width only. The general document workspace separately checks all three nominal axes against supplied available-space constraints. Room/site dimensions, physical quantity, specified finish, price and installation clearances remain unverified. The GLB download retains the default cabinet pair, without the authored room.
