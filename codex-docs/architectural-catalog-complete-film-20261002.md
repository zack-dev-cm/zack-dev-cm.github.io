# Complete architectural catalog film — 2 October 2026

The 3D story now follows eight distinct stages through the final room: original
drawing scan, native-text extraction, exact catalog match, exploded inspection,
evidence/dimension review, recorded ImageGen reveal, room construction and fit,
then the evidence handoff with the cabinet retained in the room.

The film is 64 seconds, 1920×1080, 24 fps and 1,536 frames. The homepage preview
is 1280×720 with square pixels. Both use the same deterministic interactive
scene; captions and all eight chapter controls follow its timeline. Six original
source/render PNGs are byte-identical to the published functional examples.

The room is authored. Its adjustable bay checks nominal cabinet width only.
A 48-inch cabinet is 1219.2 mm wide: a 1000 mm bay is 219.2 mm short, while a
1300 mm bay leaves 40.4 mm per side. Site dimensions, installation clearances,
physical quantity, specified finish and price remain unresolved. The recorded
ImageGen images remain the actual 36-inch oak examples.
