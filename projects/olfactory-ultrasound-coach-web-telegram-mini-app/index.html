Olfactory Ultrasound Coach (Web + Telegram Mini App)

Mini app + Web Audio coach that mirrors the olfactory tFUS presets with placement guide and safety rails.

Overview

Telegram mini app plus web placement coach for the olfactory tFUS pilot. Ships the doc's 1200 Hz pulse trains on an 18 - 20 kHz carrier, placement walk-through (50 - 55 deg tilt, +/-2 - 4 mm steer), gain/duty/session caps, logging, and the reference illustration for quick setup.

What It Covers

  • Preset pulse trains for the four reported scent sensations
  • Placement guide with tilt/steer coaching and safety caps
  • Telegram bot that opens the mini app + reference guide
