Source notes

Catalog facts, visible in 3D.

The study reuses the original documents recovered with the architectural matching project. The Case Systems product catalog has 306 pages; B3000 and B3100 appear on pages 27 and 28. Their diagrams establish one full-width upper drawer versus two side-by-side upper drawers. Both entries list two hinged doors and one adjustable shelf.

B3000 is a visible callout on page 1 of the original elevation template; B3100 appears on page 3. The source sheets stay in the project workspace. This public study redraws the cabinet families procedurally.

Dimensions and geometry

The initial 36 W × 34 H × 24 D inch size is a catalog-allowed nominal configuration. The width control uses a reviewed subset of options available for both families. These are selected catalog sizes. Installed dimensions, physical quantities and prices remain unknown.

Finish, hardware shape, panel construction, clearances and door/drawer motion are authored to make inspection readable. The downloadable GLB uses metres, with the nominal inch configuration in its metadata. It is a visualization, not a fabrication model.

Source and verification

The reviewed fact record identifies the recovered catalog by SHA-256. The browser verifies that record's checksum before showing the scene. The manufacturer's catalog resources provide the current edition, which may have different pagination and specifications.

The scene uses locally hosted Three.js r180 and OrbitControls. The 30-second film is captured from the same scene at fixed times; it does not run an OCR or vision model. Document matching and review are explained through the original code/catalog examples.
